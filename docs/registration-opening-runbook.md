# Future registration opening runbook

This is a sanitized planning checklist, not authorisation to open registration. Production must remain `closed` until the organiser explicitly approves a future deployment.

Before changing the server configuration from `closed` to `open`, record approval for all of the following:

1. Race permissions and approval are current.
2. Opening date and time are confirmed.
3. The privacy notice covers the final fields, purposes, recipients and retention.
4. Race terms are approved and versioned.
5. Payment provider, fees, settlement and reconciliation are tested.
6. Transactional-email sender and templates are approved and tested.
7. Production storage, deletion, backup and recovery are tested.
8. Organiser authentication, authorization and access review are complete.
9. Capacity and waiting-list policy are approved.
10. Refund, cancellation and transfer policies are approved.
11. Monitoring, support contacts and incident handling are ready.
12. Automated, privacy, concurrency, accessibility and browser tests pass.
13. The organiser gives explicit go-live approval.
14. A reviewed server-side configuration change sets production from `closed` to `open`.
15. A controlled post-deployment submission is completed and reconciled.

If any check is incomplete, leave production closed. Do not use a date, URL parameter or client-side setting as an opening mechanism.

Detailed operational and security review information is maintained separately from the public website.
