# 2026 content plan

This plan records information that must be confirmed before it is presented as
current guidance for the 2026 Blorenge Fell Race. It contains no private
organiser or stakeholder contact details.

## Confirmed public information

- Event: Blorenge Fell Race
- Date: Saturday 28 November 2026
- Race start: 1:00 pm
- Race HQ: Llanfoist Community Hall
- Race start location: bottom of Church Lane, Llanfoist
- Distance: 5.7 km
- Ascent: 485 m
- Entry fee: £6
- Entry limit: 110 runners
- Minimum age: 16
- No entries on the day
- Parking is available but limited
- Walking, cycling, public transport and lift-sharing are encouraged
