Blorenge Fell Race

Race information

Travel for 2026

Race HQ is at Llanfoist Community Hall. The race itself starts at the bottom of Church Lane, Llanfoist.

Rail and bus

Abergavenny is the nearest railway station. Check current onward bus and local travel options with Traveline Cymru before setting out; timetables can change.

Walking and cycling

Walking from Abergavenny or Llanfoist may be practical for some participants. Cycle parking is available at Llanfoist Community Hall. Plan for winter weather, visibility and the return journey.

Driving

Parking is available but limited. Please lift-share where possible, park considerately and follow any race-day directions from marshals.

Choose the safest practical option for your circumstances. Check live public-transport information and weather before travelling.

2026 kit requirements

Bring every item on the full kit list to the event, even if the weather looks settled. The Race Organiser will decide on the day what competitors must carry.

  • Waterproof/windproof top
  • Waterproof/windproof legs
  • Hat
  • Gloves
  • Compass
  • Whistle
  • Food and drink as needed for the conditions
  • A waterproofed map of the race route
  • Something to carry the kit in, such as a bumbag or rucksack

Practical guidance

Wear fell-running shoes with effective grip and arrive prepared for exposed, wet and cold winter conditions. Competitors are responsible for their own safety and must follow the Race Organiser’s instructions.

Navigation must not rely solely on a phone, watch or other electronic device. Bring the map and compass, know how to use them and read the Welsh Fell Runners Association guidance for runners before race day.

Do not leave kit at home. The on-the-day decision is about what you must carry during the race, not what you need to bring to registration.
