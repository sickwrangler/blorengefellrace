Race information

Travel for 2026

Race HQ is at Llanfoist Community Hall. The race itself starts at the bottom of Church Lane, Llanfoist.

Parking is available but limited. Please walk, cycle, use public transport or lift-share where possible.

Detailed public-transport directions will be published before entries open.

2026 kit requirements

The mandatory and recommended kit requirements for 2026 have not yet been confirmed. Further details will be published before entries open.

Environment award winner 2025

2024 was the first time the Blorenge Fell Race was organised, and the organisers were honoured to win the WFRA Environment Award.

We are committed to making this event as low impact as possible and will continue to improve our practices year on year. We would like to thank everyone who took part in the event and helped us achieve this award.

“Incredible efforts to minimise the impact of their race on, and promote awareness of, the environment and ways in which we as runners and ROs can ‘do our bit’. They had thought of everything […] Even more incredible is that they were first-time ROs. The fell running world needs more Patricks and Kanis!”
Welsh fell runners Environment awards 2025
