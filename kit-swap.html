Good kit deserves more miles

Bring something, take something Dewch â rhywbeth, ewch â rhywbeth

Bring clean running or fell kit that you no longer need but that still has useful life in it. Leave it for another runner, browse what others have brought and take something that will be useful to you.

The swap is informal and free. You do not have to bring an item before taking one. Particular items and sizes cannot be guaranteed, but sharing what we already have is a practical way to reduce waste and support our running community.

Please bring only clean, usable kit that you would be happy to pass on to a friend.
