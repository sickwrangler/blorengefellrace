Care for the mountain

Amgylchedd
Environment

The Blorenge is much more than the backdrop to this race. It is a place many of us run, walk and return to throughout the year. Seeing the damage caused by serious wildfires around Blaenavon and the surrounding hills in 2026 was deeply upsetting. These are landscapes and habitats that local people, runners and visitors care about.

We hope the race encourages people not only to enjoy these hills, but to value and look after them too.

We continue to support Stump Up For Trees, a local community charity creating native woodland and improving biodiversity across this part of Wales. If you would like to help, please consider learning more, volunteering or making a donation.
